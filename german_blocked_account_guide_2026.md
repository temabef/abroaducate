## 1. What is a German Blocked Account and How Much is Required in 2026?

A **Blocked Account (*Sperrkonto*)** is a specialized bank account legally recognized by the German Federal Foreign Office (*Auswärtiges Amt*). It ensures that international students have sufficient funds to cover accommodation, food, health insurance, and living expenses during their studies.

### The 2026 Legal Requirements:
* **Total Annual Blocked Amount:** **€11,904** for 12 months
* **Monthly Disbursement Allowance:** **€992 per month**
* **Additional Buffer Deposit:** Most providers require a small buffer (~€100) to cover international wire transfer fees and currency fluctuations, which is refunded to you upon your first payout.

> **Important:** Once deposited, the money is locked until you physically land in Germany, register your address (*Anmeldung*), and activate your monthly payouts to a local German checking account (*Girokonto*).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens to my money if my visa is rejected?
Your funds are **100% safe and legally protected**. If your visa is denied, simply upload your official embassy rejection letter to your Expatrio or Fintiba portal, and the full blocked amount will be refunded directly back to the original bank account.

### Can I withdraw more than €992 per month?
No. Under German immigration law, you cannot exceed the €992 monthly withdrawal limit. This regulation protects students from running out of living funds before the academic year ends.

### Can I work while studying in Germany?
Yes! International students from non-EU countries are legally permitted to work **140 full days or 280 half days per calendar year**, or take up a "working student" (*Werkstudent*) job, allowing you to supplement your blocked account income significantly.
