For international students aiming to build careers in computer science, software engineering, business analytics, and artificial intelligence, two East Coast technological universities stand out as premier destinations: **Northeastern University** (Boston, Massachusetts) and **Stevens Institute of Technology** (Hoboken, New Jersey).

Both institutions are renowned for their direct integration with tech industry employers, robust Curricular Practical Training (CPT) and Co-op programs, and proximity to major metropolitan technology corridors (Boston and the greater New York City metropolitan area).

However, submitting applications to both institutions requires upfront payments:
- **Northeastern University:** $75 to $100 for graduate degree applications.
- **Stevens Institute of Technology:** $60 to $80 for graduate degree applications.

Submitting dossiers to both schools can cost nearly $180 before factoring in transcript dispatches or standardized score reports.

Fortunately, both Northeastern and Stevens maintain generous recruitment fee waiver programs that allow international applicants to bypass these application fees completely.

Below, we detail how to qualify for application fee waivers at Northeastern University and Stevens Institute of Technology, explain how virtual open houses yield waiver codes, and outline step-by-step submission instructions.

## Part 1: Securing an Application Fee Waiver at Northeastern University

![Study desk flatlay with admissions checklists for Northeastern and Stevens, transcripts, and tablet portal]({{BODY_IMAGE_URL}})

Northeastern University—famous for its Khoury College of Computer Sciences, College of Engineering, and D'Amore-McKim School of Business—handles graduate admissions across specialized colleges:

### 1. Attending Virtual Graduate Open Houses & Preview Days
Throughout the autumn and winter admissions seasons, Northeastern colleges host **Virtual Graduate Preview Webinars**:
- The Khoury College of Computer Sciences and College of Engineering regularly grant **single-use fee waiver codes** to prospective students who attend live informational webinars.
- **Action Plan:** Visit Northeastern's Graduate Admissions events page, register with the exact email address you plan to use for your application portal, and attend the entire session. Waiver codes or automated fee exemptions are typically distributed during the session or sent via email within 48 to 72 hours.

### 2. Departmental Priority Application Cycles
Certain Northeastern graduate programs host "Early Application Priority Windows" where the application fee is automatically waived for all applicants who submit their dossier before designated early-bird deadlines.

### 3. Pipeline Partnerships & Diversity Fellowships
Northeastern provides automatic fee waivers to candidates affiliated with partner organizations, including:
- GEM Fellowship applicants
- McNair Scholars
- Fulbright Scholars
- SACNAS and ABRCMS conference attendees

## Part 2: Securing an Application Fee Waiver at Stevens Institute of Technology

Stevens Institute of Technology, located directly across the Hudson River from Manhattan, is a top-tier private research university renowned for financial engineering, cybersecurity, computer science, and systems engineering.

Stevens offers several direct pathways for international graduate applicants to waive the application fee:

### 1. Stevens Virtual Graduate Information Sessions
Stevens conducts regular online information sessions led by graduate admissions counselors:
- International attendees are routinely provided with a **promotional application fee waiver code** during the Q&A segment of the presentation.
- **Action Plan:** Monitor the Stevens Graduate Admissions calendar. Register and participate in an online information session or academic department webinar prior to submitting your application.

### 2. Priority Recruitment & Corporate Partner Waivers
Stevens partners with international academic institutions and professional engineering organizations worldwide. In addition, Stevens frequently hosts "Free Application Weeks" during late autumn and early winter, during which the application fee is automatically zeroed out in the admissions checkout screen for all submitted applications.

## How to Apply Waiver Codes in the Admissions Portals

Both Northeastern and Stevens utilize the Slate admissions portal system:

1. **Complete Every Form Section:** Complete your personal details, academic history, resume, and statement of purpose.
2. **Assign Recommenders:** Input referee details so recommendation links are dispatched.
3. **Navigate to the Payment Page:** Select **Application Fee Waiver Code** or **Promotional Code**.
4. **Input the Alphanumeric Code:** Enter your verified waiver code and click **Apply**.
5. **Confirm $0.00 Balance:** Verify that your balance due reflects **$0.00** before clicking the final Submit button.
6. **Important Policy Reminder:** Neither institution issues refunds for application fees already paid. Do not submit your application with a credit card if you are awaiting a waiver code confirmation.

## Required Application Documents

To ensure your application is competitive for admission and institutional scholarships:

1. **Official Transcripts & Degree Certificates:** Scanned copies of your semester mark sheets and degree certificate. Documents in languages other than English must be accompanied by certified translations. You can secure certified academic translations accepted by US graduate admissions committees through [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).
2. **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** A focused 1 to 2-page essay highlighting your academic background, technical skills, and clear reasons for choosing Northeastern's or Stevens' specialized curriculum. Refine and structure your essay using the [Abroaducate SOP Builder](/sop).
3. **Letters of Recommendation:** 2 to 3 strong letters from academic professors or technical managers.
4. **Resume / Curriculum Vitae:** Detailing technical proficiencies (Python, Java, C++, cloud platforms), engineering projects, and internships.
5. **English Proficiency Scores:** TOEFL iBT, IELTS Academic, or Duolingo English Test (depending on departmental requirements).

## Financial Planning & Virtual USD Payments

Both Boston and the NYC/Hoboken metropolitan areas have higher living costs than rural US college towns. Graduate students typically budget between **$1,500 and $2,200 per month** for housing, utilities, food, and public transit.

Incoming students must demonstrate financial resources during the Form I-20 student visa process.

When paying enrollment deposits or managing international wire transfers, domestic bank debit cards from Africa, Asia, or South America often fail due to international transaction limits.
