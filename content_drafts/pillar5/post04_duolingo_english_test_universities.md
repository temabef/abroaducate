For international students planning to pursue graduate study in the United States, standardized English proficiency exams have historically represented one of the most frustrating and expensive barriers to entry.

Traditional English exams like the **TOEFL iBT** and **IELTS Academic** typically cost between **$220 and $260** per test attempt. In addition, scheduling a test slot often requires traveling to designated physical test centers in major cities, waiting up to two weeks for score processing, and paying **$25 to $35** for each additional university score report beyond the first four.

If an applicant applies to six or seven universities, testing and reporting costs can easily exceed **$400**.

To eliminate this logistical and financial friction, thousands of accredited American universities now officially accept the **Duolingo English Test (DET)**:
- **Affordable Testing Fee:** The DET costs only **$59** (or two tests for $98).
- **Take from Home Anytime:** The entire test is administered online via your computer with a webcam and microphone, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
- **Fast 48-Hour Results:** Official certified scores are delivered within 48 hours.
- **Unlimited Free Score Reports:** You can send your official DET score reports to an **unlimited number of universities for free** with a single click.

Crucially: **Taking the Duolingo English Test does not hurt your chances of winning graduate assistantships or merit scholarships.** Admitted students who meet published departmental DET thresholds are fully eligible for Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) and Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA).

Below, we detail 10 top accredited US universities that accept the Duolingo English Test for Master's admissions, outline minimum score requirements, and explain how to prepare.

## 3. How to Prepare for the Duolingo English Test

While the DET is shorter (typically 1 hour) than IELTS or TOEFL (3 hours), its computer-adaptive format requires focused practice:

1. **Computer-Adaptive Algorithm:** Questions adjust in difficulty based on your previous answers. If you answer correctly, subsequent questions become more complex.
2. **Four Integrated Subscores:** Literacy (reading & writing), Comprehension (reading & listening), Conversation (listening & speaking), and Production (writing & speaking).
3. **Interactive Speaking & Ungraded Video Interview:** Admissions committees receive your scores alongside a 2-minute video interview recording, allowing faculty to assess your spoken fluency and communicative confidence directly.
4. **Targeted Practice:** Practicing with a native English speaker via 1-on-1 tutoring on [Preply](https://preply.com/en/?pref=NTI0MTQ1Nw==&id=1729007621.727764) can significantly boost your Production and Conversation subscores.
