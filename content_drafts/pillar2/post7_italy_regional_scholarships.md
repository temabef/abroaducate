Italy has quietly become one of the most generous study destinations in Europe for international students from low- and middle-income families. While public university tuition in Italy is already modest compared to the United States or the United Kingdom (typically ranging from 1,000 EUR to 4,000 EUR per year based on family income), the country's constitutional mandate to support higher education for economically disadvantaged students unlocks an even greater benefit: regional study grants (*Borse di Studio Regionali*).

Every administrative region in Italy operates a dedicated agency for the Right to Higher Education (*Ente per il Diritto allo Studio Universitario*). These agencies distribute need-based scholarships that cover 100% of university tuition fees, provide free accommodation in student residences or a cash housing supplement, offer free daily meals at campus cafeterias (*mense*), and grant an annual cash stipend of up to 8,500 EUR paid directly into the student's Italian bank account.

Because these scholarships are awarded based on household economic circumstances rather than past grade point averages, international students from countries with lower currency exchange rates frequently qualify for the maximum scholarship bracket.

In this guide, we break down the major Italian regional scholarship bodies, explain how the financial evaluation indicator (ISEE Parificato) is calculated, outline required documents, and walk you through the key application steps for the 2026/2027 academic year.

## What Does an Italian Regional Scholarship Include?

A full regional scholarship generally divides benefits across four main categories:

1. **Tuition Fee Exemption:** Complete waiver of university enrollment taxes and regional student contributions.
2. **Student Accommodation:** A reserved room in an official regional student residence hall (*Collegio / Residenza Universitaria*). If no dorm room is available due to high demand, the agency converts the room benefit into a monthly cash housing subsidy.
3. **Campus Dining (Mensa):** One to two free meals per day at contracted university cafeterias throughout the academic term.
4. **Annual Cash Stipend:** A direct cash disbursement distributed in two installments (typically December/January and June/July). The total stipend ranges between 5,500 EUR and 8,500 EUR per year depending on whether the student is classified as *in sede* (local), *pendolare* (commuter), or *fuori sede* (non-resident/international). International students whose families live outside Italy are automatically classified as *fuori sede*, qualifying for the highest funding tier.

## The Core Metric: Understanding ISEE Parificato

Italian regional scholarships are strictly need-based. Eligibility is evaluated using a standardized financial index called the **ISEE Parificato** (*Indicatore della Situazione Economica Equivalente Parificato*), calculated specifically for foreign students whose family income and assets originate outside Italy.

To qualify for the full scholarship and housing, your ISEE Parificato must generally fall below an established regional threshold, typically between **23,000 EUR and 27,000 EUR** (the exact ceiling is published annually in each agency's official call for applications, or *Bando di Concorso*).

The ISEE Parificato takes into account:
* **Family Composition:** The number of individuals living in the household.
* **Annual Gross Income:** Total earnings of all adult household members during the preceding calendar year.
* **Real Estate Assets:** Ownership of the family home or any other property, measured in square meters.
* **Movable Assets:** Total savings, checking balances, and financial holdings as of December 31 of the previous year.

Because local salaries in many developing economies convert to low euro amounts, international students from regions like South Asia, Africa, and Latin America frequently fall well beneath the 23,000 EUR ceiling, qualifying for the maximum award level.

## Complete Document Checklist for ISEE Parificato

Calculating your ISEE Parificato requires obtaining specific legal documents from your home country, having them officially translated, and legalizing them through local government authorities and the Italian consulate.

### Required Household Documents
1. **Family Composition Certificate:** An official civil registry document or family census certificate listing every member of your household, their dates of birth, and their relationship to you.
2. **Income Certificate:** Official tax returns, employer salary slips, or pension certificates for each working member of the family for the reference calendar year. If an adult household member is unemployed, an official certificate of unemployment must be provided.
3. **Property/Real Estate Certificate:** Official documentation stating whether the family owns their residence, along with the total square meters of the property. If the family rents, a copy of the official lease agreement showing monthly rental payments is required.
4. **Bank and Movable Assets Statement:** Bank balance certificates for all adult household members detailing account balances as of December 31.

### Legalization and Translation Protocol
Documents originating outside Italy cannot simply be submitted in their original language:
* **Sworn/Certified Italian Translation:** All non-Italian documents must be translated into Italian. Admissions offices and Italian CAF (*Centro di Assistenza Fiscale*) offices reject uncertified drafts. For fast, official translations that meet Italian consular standards, services like [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd) provide certified document translations formatted to satisfy European administrative requirements.
* **Apostille or Consular Legalization:** If your home country is a signatory to the Hague Apostille Convention, your original documents must carry an official Apostille stamp. If your country is not part of the convention, your documents must undergo consular legalization (*Legalizzazione Consolare*) at the Italian Embassy or Consulate in your country of origin.
* **Submission to an Italian CAF:** Once translated and legalized, your dossier is submitted to an authorized Italian tax assistance center (*CAF*) either online or upon arrival in Italy to generate your formal ISEE Parificato certificate.

## How to Maintain Your Scholarship in Subsequent Years

Unlike the first academic year, where regional scholarships are awarded almost entirely on economic need, maintaining your funding in subsequent years requires fulfilling **academic merit requirements (CFU credits)**:

* **First-Year Renewal:** Students must obtain a minimum number of European Credit Transfer System (ECTS/CFU) credits—typically between 20 and 35 credits depending on the region—by August 10 or November 30 of their first year.
* **Bonus Credits:** Most regional agencies provide a one-time pool of bonus credits (typically 5 to 15 CFU) that can be used once during your degree if you fall slightly short of the credit threshold.
* **Degree Completion:** Students who fail to obtain the required minimum credits within the academic year may be required to repay all or part of the financial aid received.

## 2026/2027 Italian Regional Scholarship Timeline

* **May – June 2026:** Gather home country income, property, and family composition documents. Begin the legalization and sworn translation process early to avoid summer embassy backlogs.
* **July – August 2026:** Regional agencies publish their annual calls for applications (*Bandi di Concorso*). Complete online registration on the regional portal (DSU, EDISU, ER.GO, or LazioDisco) and upload initial digital documents. Deadlines typically close between late August and mid-September.
* **September – October 2026:** Provisional ranking lists (*Graduatorie Provvisorie*) are published. Review your status to ensure you are listed as an eligible candidate (*Idoneo*).
* **November – December 2026:** Final ranking lists (*Graduatorie Definitive*) are published. The first installment of the cash scholarship is disbursed, and cafeteria access cards are activated.
* **June – July 2027:** The second scholarship installment is transferred upon verification of Spring semester academic credit progression.
