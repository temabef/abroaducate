For graduates holding a **Higher National Diploma (HND)** from polytechnics in Nigeria, Ghana, the UK, or other Commonwealth educational systems, pursuing postgraduate education abroad has historically been fraught with ambiguity.

In many domestic and international systems, HND holders are routinely told that their diploma is insufficient for direct Master's admission, forcing them into one-to-two-year "top-up" Bachelor's degrees or mandatory Post Graduate Diplomas (PGD) before they can even be considered for graduate study.

However, the United States higher education system operates under a vastly more flexible, credit-based framework. 

In the US, graduate admission is not governed by rigid civil-service degree hierarchies. Instead, US admissions committees evaluate your total completed semester credit hours, academic coursework distribution, and the official assessment of professional credential evaluation agencies (such as World Education Services, ECE, or Josef Silny & Associates).

When evaluated through the appropriate channels, **a completed HND combined with an Ordinary National Diploma (OND) or relevant professional post-diploma experience can be evaluated as equivalent to a four-year US Bachelor's degree**, unlocking direct entry into Master of Science (MS), Master of Arts (MA), and MBA programs.

Below, we detail the accredited US universities that accept HND credentials for direct graduate admission, explain how credential evaluations work, and provide the exact steps to build a winning admissions dossier.

---

## 1. How US Universities Evaluate the HND Credential

In most Commonwealth systems, the polytechnic journey consists of two years of National Diploma (OND/ND), a mandatory one-year industrial training (IT) internship, and two years of Higher National Diploma (HND) coursework.

When an independent evaluation agency (such as WES or ECE) reviews this five-year academic and professional progression:
- **Total Credit Equivalency:** The combined OND and HND curricula often tally between **120 and 150 semester credit hours**, easily meeting or exceeding the 120-credit threshold required for a standard four-year US Bachelor's degree.
- **The WES Evaluation Outcome:** WES frequently evaluates a completed 2-year ND + 2-year HND from an accredited polytechnic as equivalent to a **"Bachelor's degree (four years)"** or **"four years of undergraduate study"** in the United States.
- **Direct Master's Eligibility:** Once an evaluation report confirms four-year US Bachelor's equivalency, university graduate admission offices can legally process the candidate for direct Master's admission without requiring prerequisite bridge diplomas.

## 2. Accredited US Universities Accepting HND Holders for Direct Master's

The following accredited US universities maintain published policies or verified precedent admitting HND graduates directly into Master's degree programs, provided they submit an approved credential evaluation report:

### 3. East Tennessee State University (ETSU)

### 4. Texas A&M University–Commerce
- **Policy:** Evaluates HND credentials through official external evaluation reports.
- **Programs Available:** MS in Computer Science, Business Analytics, and Technology Management.
- **Advantage:** Exceptional STEM OPT job placement within the Dallas-Fort Worth technology corridor.

### 5. Central Michigan University (CMU)
- **Policy:** Considers HND holders who demonstrate four-year US equivalency through WES or ECE.
- **Programs Available:** MS in Information Systems, MS in Computer Science, and Master of Science in Administration.

### 6. Southern Illinois University Edwardsville (SIUE)
- **Policy:** Accepts HND holders for direct graduate admissions across select engineering and applied science tracks with an approved NACES course-by-course evaluation.

- **Policy:** Evaluates international polytechnic diplomas for direct entry into Master of Science in Information Studies, Engineering Management, and MBA programs.
- **Advantage:** Practical Curricular Practical Training (CPT) integration for working professionals.

## 3. The 4-Step Process to Apply with an HND

To ensure your application is reviewed smoothly without administrative delays:

### Step 1: Order a WES or ECE Course-by-Course Evaluation
Do not submit your raw polytechnic transcripts directly to a university admissions portal without first determining their evaluation requirements.
- Order a **WES Course-by-Course (ICAP)** or **ECE Course-by-Course** evaluation.
- Request that your polytechnic registrar send both your **OND and HND official transcripts** directly to WES or ECE in a sealed envelope or via secure institutional electronic dispatch.
- Ensure the evaluation covers both diplomas together to demonstrate the full four-year progression.

### Step 2: Prepare Certified Document Translations (If Applicable)
If your diploma, certificates, or transcripts were issued in a language other than English, you must include certified word-for-word translations. You can order certified academic translations meeting US university standards through [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).

### Step 3: Craft a Strategic Statement of Purpose
Your Statement of Purpose should highlight your hands-on technical skills. Polytechnic education emphasizes rigorous laboratory coursework and practical industrial internships (IT), giving you a tangible edge over purely theoretical candidates. Frame your practical engineering or technical background as an asset for applied graduate research. You can structure your essay with the [Abroaducate SOP Builder](/sop).

### Step 4: Secure Strong Academic and Professional Recommendations
Obtain 2 to 3 letters of recommendation:
- One from a polytechnic senior lecturer or department head.
- One or two from industry supervisors during your Industrial Training or post-HND employment.
