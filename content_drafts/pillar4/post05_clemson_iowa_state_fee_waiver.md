For prospective international graduate students in engineering, computer science, agriculture, and physical sciences, two public R1 land-grant research universities consistently rank among the most desirable in the United States: **Clemson University** (in South Carolina) and **Iowa State University** (in Ames, Iowa).

Both institutions provide exceptional laboratory facilities, substantial industry partnerships, and high graduate assistantship placement rates.

However, each application carries a standard fee:
- **Clemson University:** $90 to $100 for international graduate applications.
- **Iowa State University:** $100 for international graduate applications.

Submitting applications to both institutions represents an immediate out-of-pocket expense of nearly $200. For international students operating with tight budgets, fee waivers can free up vital resources for visa fees, standardized tests, and credential evaluations.

In this guide, we analyze the official pathways through which international applicants can secure application fee waivers at Clemson University and Iowa State University.

---

## Part 1: Securing a Graduate Application Fee Waiver at Clemson University

![Overhead flatlay of university graduate application files, transcripts, calculator, and notebook on a wooden desk]({{BODY_IMAGE_URL}})

Graduate admissions at Clemson are administered through the Graduate School, with substantial discretion granted to individual departments within the College of Engineering, Computing and Applied Sciences (CECAS) and College of Science.

### 1. Departmental Info Sessions & Virtual Preview Days
Throughout October, November, and December, several engineering departments at Clemson host **virtual graduate information sessions**. 
- Departments such as Electrical & Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Automotive Engineering frequently distribute promotional **fee waiver promo codes** to attendees.
- **Action:** Visit Clemson's Graduate School recruitment calendar and register with the exact email address you intend to use on Clemson's Slate application portal. Attend the entire session to qualify for post-event code emails.

### 2. Faculty Sponsorship (Graduate Research Assistantships)
Because Clemson is an intensive research hub, faculty principal investigators (PIs) who hold active National Science Foundation (NSF) or industry research grants often have the institutional authority to request fee waivers for prospective PhD or research Master's candidates.
- If your undergraduate research background, publication history, or technical skillset directly matches an open faculty lab vacancy, you can reach out to the professor via a well-structured research inquiry email. If the professor wishes to interview you, they can request that the Graduate School waive your application submission fee.

### 3. Diversity and National Pipeline Programs
Clemson Graduate School automatically grants application fee waivers to participants of recognized pipeline organizations, including:
- GEM Fellowship participants and associate fellows
- McNair Scholars
- SACNAS (Society for the Advancement of Chicanos/Hispanics and Native Americans in Science)
- ABRCMS attendees

## Part 2: Securing a Graduate Application Fee Waiver at Iowa State University

Iowa State University (ISU) is world-renowned for agricultural and biosystems engineering, materials science, statistics, and aerospace engineering.

ISU admissions operates through the Office of Admissions and individual graduate programs:

### 1. The Pre-Application & Preliminary Review Pathway
One of the most cost-effective features of Iowa State University is that several prominent departments offer a **free preliminary review (pre-application)**:
- **How It Works:** Before paying the $100 application fee, departments like Computer Science, Electrical & Computer Engineering, and Statistics permit international applicants to submit an informal pre-application consisting of scanned transcripts, resume, and statement of purpose at **$0 cost**.
- **The Outcome:** The departmental admissions committee reviews your profile. If your profile demonstrates a strong likelihood of admission and funding, they issue you a formal invitation to submit the official university application—often accompanied by a departmental fee sponsorship or voucher code.

### 2. Departmental Graduate Information Webinars
Similar to Clemson, Iowa State's College of Engineering and College of Liberal Arts and Sciences conduct online recruitment webinars during the autumn admissions season. Participating international candidates are often eligible for institutional waiver vouchers upon attending the live presentations.

### 3. GEM and International Exchange Affiliations
Iowa State is a GEM member institution and maintains bilateral exchange partnerships with select international research universities. Scholars affiliated with designated governmental sponsorship programs or partner institutes are exempt from application fees.

## How to Enter Waiver Codes in the Admissions Portals

Both Clemson and Iowa State process applications through secure digital portals:

1. **Complete Every Form Section:** Enter your personal information, undergraduate coursework, recommendation contacts, and essay submissions first.
2. **Review for Verification:** Once you reach the checkout screen, select **Fee Waiver Code** or **Promotional Voucher**.
3. **Validate $0 Balance:** Enter your code and click apply. Verify that your total amount due displays **$0.00** before clicking submit.
4. **Important Warning:** Admissions portals do not issue retroactive refunds. If you submit with a credit card before a waiver is validated, the university will not reimburse the fee.

## Required Application Documents

To ensure your application is competitive for admission and graduate assistantships:

1. **Certified Academic Transcripts:** Scanned copies of your semester mark sheets and degree certificate. Non-English credentials require certified translations. You can obtain certified academic translations compliant with US graduate admissions through [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).
2. **Statement of Purpose:** A targeted 1 to 2-page essay detailing your research background, technical achievements, and clear alignment with specific faculty research groups. You can refine your essay with the [Abroaducate SOP Builder](/sop).
3. **Letters of Recommendation:** 3 letters from professors or supervisors who can speak in detail about your quantitative and research capabilities.
4. **Resume / Academic CV:** Highlighting technical skills (e.g., Python, MATLAB, CAD, R), projects, and publications.
5. **English Proficiency Scores:** TOEFL iBT, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test (depending on departmental minimums).

## Financial Proof & Cross-Border Money Transfers

Once admitted to Clemson or Iowa State, you will need to demonstrate proof of financial support to obtain your Form I-20:
- If you receive a Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA) or Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA), your tuition is typically waived and you receive an annual stipend of **$22,000 to $32,000**, which satisfies the majority of I-20 financial requirements.
- For self-funded portions, campus housing deposits, and international travel logistics, traditional bank transfers from overseas can incur heavy currency markups and processing delays.
