When budgeting for a graduate degree in the United States, prospective students often calculate the cost of tuition, housing, and health insurance. Yet before an applicant ever receives an acceptance letter, the initial application fees alone can consume a substantial portion of an international student's savings.

Standard US application fees typically range from $70 to $130 per institution. For an applicant submitting dossiers to six or seven universities, application costs frequently surpass $600.

Fortunately, several established American research institutions maintain open-access policies designed to eliminate this friction. Among the most popular and academically versatile is the **University of Dayton**.

Located in Dayton, Ohio, the University of Dayton is a top-tier national Catholic research university recognized for its School of Engineering, School of Business Administration, and College of Arts and Sciences. 

Crucially for international applicants: **the University of Dayton charges an automatic $0 application fee for all graduate degree applications.**

You do not need a special coupon code, a hardship waiver form, or an invitation from an alumni network. When you complete your online application through Dayton's institutional portal, the submission fee is automatically zeroed out.

Below, we detail Dayton's top English-taught graduate programs, funding and scholarship avenues, required application documents, and step-by-step submission instructions.

---

## The $0 Application Fee Policy: How It Works

Many US universities advertise application fee waivers but restrict them to domestic students with FAFSA records or individuals who attend specific live webinars.

The University of Dayton approaches graduate admissions differently:

1. **Permanently Waived:** The $0 fee is not a limited-time promotional campaign; it is a permanent structural policy across the University of Dayton Graduate School.
2. **All International Students Qualify:** Whether you are applying from Nigeria, India, Ghana, Vietnam, Kenya, or Pakistan, your application fee is automatically $0.
3. **No Financial Paperwork Needed at Application:** You do not need to provide bank statements, tax returns, or employment records during the initial submission phase to claim the $0 fee.

- **Degree Offered:** Master of Science and Doctor of Philosophy.

- **Degree Offered:** Master of Business Administration (MBA) and MS in Business Analytics.

## Graduate Scholarships & Assistantship Funding

While the application itself is free, securing financial support is critical for international scholars:

### 1. Departmental Graduate Assistantships (GTA & GRA)
Academic departments at Dayton offer competitive Graduate Assistantships that provide:
- **Partial or Full Tuition Waivers:** Remitting tuition credits for full-time graduate enrollment.
- **Monthly Living Stipends:** Salaried stipends earned through 15 to 20 hours per week of research or teaching duties.
- **Application Process:** Applicants indicate their interest in an assistantship directly on their main admissions application. Early submission (before **February 1** for Fall intake) is strongly recommended.

### 2. International Graduate Merit Scholarships
The University of Dayton Graduate School awards merit-based tuition discounts and scholarships to high-achieving international applicants based on cumulative undergraduate GPA and technical background. These awards reduce per-credit tuition costs across your degree program without requiring separate applications.

## Required Documentation Checklist

Because the application is free, Dayton's admissions committee conducts a thorough holistic review of academic records. Prepare clean digital scans of the following materials:

1. **Academic Transcripts & Degree Certificates:** Scanned copies of your official undergraduate transcripts covering all semesters, along with your graduation diploma or provisional certificate.
2. **Certified Document Translations:** If your academic records, certificates, or transcripts were issued in a language other than English, you must upload certified, word-for-word translations. You can secure official certified academic translations accepted by US graduate admissions boards through [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).
3. **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** A well-structured 1 to 2-page essay highlighting your academic foundations, professional goals, and why Dayton's curriculum matches your career trajectory. You can draft, refine, and structure your essay using the [Abroaducate SOP Builder](/sop).
4. **Letters of Recommendation:** Typically 2 to 3 academic or professional letters submitted digitally by your referees.
5. **Resume / Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Highlighting research projects, internships, leadership roles, and technical tools (e.g., Python, SQL, CAD, R).
6. **English Language Proficiency Scores:** Accepted exams include TOEFL iBT (minimum 80), IELTS (minimum 6.5), or Duolingo English Test (minimum 115). Dayton also offers conditional admission through its Intensive English Program (IEP) for qualified students who need language preparation.
