Applying to graduate engineering and STEM programs in the United States often requires hundreds of dollars in upfront fees before your application is even evaluated. With average graduate application fees hovering between $75 and $125 per school, building a balanced portfolio of five or six institutions quickly becomes an expensive financial hurdle for international candidates.

However, a select group of prominent US research universities have eliminated this barrier entirely. Leading this model is **Michigan Technological University (Michigan Tech)**.

Located in Houghton on Michigan's Upper Peninsula, Michigan Tech is a nationally recognized public research institution celebrated for its rigorous curricula in mechanical engineering, computing, robotics, materials science, and environmental engineering. 

Best of all for prospective graduate scholars: **Michigan Tech charges an automatic $0 application fee for all graduate degree applications.**

Unlike universities that require complex hardship petitions, attendance at virtual open houses, or obscure promo codes, Michigan Tech applies this policy automatically to every applicant, including all international students applying from abroad.

This guide provides an exhaustive breakdown of Michigan Tech's admissions process, eligible degree departments, graduate assistantship funding opportunities, and document submission protocols.

## How the Michigan Tech $0 Application Fee Works

Most US universities use fee waivers as an exception, requiring applicants to submit income documentation or demonstrate extreme economic distress.

Michigan Tech treats the free application as a standard institutional principle:

1. **No Promo Code Required:** The graduate application portal (powered by Slate) does not request a discount code or payment authorization screen for standard graduate degree applications.
2. **Universal Eligibility:** Domestic, international, resident, and non-resident candidates are treated identically. You do not need to submit personal tax records or bank balance proofs just to submit your application.
3. **Applies to Multiple Programs:** If you wish to apply to more than one graduate program at Michigan Tech (for instance, an MS in Computer Science and an MS in Data Science), both applications are completely free of charge.

*Note: The only minor exceptions apply to specific specialized non-degree professional certificates or select executive hybrid tracks; traditional Master of Science (MS) and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programs are 100% free to submit.*

- **Degree Tracks:** MS and PhD.

- **Degree Tracks:** MS and PhD.
- **Academic Focus:** Regenerative tissue engineering, biomaterials, and optical medical devices.

## Graduate Funding: Assistantships & Fellowships

Submitting a free application is only half of the objective; winning financial backing to cover tuition and living expenses is what makes graduate school possible.

Michigan Tech funds hundreds of international graduate students each semester through three primary mechanisms:

| Funding Mechanism | Full Tuition Coverage | Monthly / Annual Stipend | Work Obligation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA)** | 100% Covered | $20,000 to $27,000 per academic year | 20 hours/week assisting faculty with undergraduate labs, grading, and recitations. |
| **Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA)** | 100% Covered | $21,000 to $28,000 per academic year | 20 hours/week conducting funded lab research under a faculty Principal Investigator (PI). |
| **Graduate School Fellowships** | Full or Partial | Varies by endowed fellowship fund | Merit awards allocated to top-ranking doctoral and master's entrants. |

### Priority Deadlines for Funding
While Michigan Tech reviews graduate applications on a rolling basis throughout the year, departmental assistantships are allocated during the initial review cycle. To maximize your chances of receiving a fully funded assistantship offer, submit your dossier before:
- **Fall Intake (August Start):** Priority funding deadline is **January 15 to February 15**.
- **Spring Intake (January Start):** Priority funding deadline is **September 1 to October 1**.

## Application Requirements & Document Checklist

Because there is no financial cost to apply, Michigan Tech maintains high academic evaluation standards. Assemble the following materials before beginning your online submission:

1. **Academic Transcripts:** Michigan Tech allows you to upload unofficial scanned copies of your degree transcripts for initial evaluation. If your transcripts were issued by an institution outside the English-speaking world, you must provide certified English translations. You can secure official, certified academic translations accepted by US graduate admissions boards through [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).
2. **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** A focused 1.5 to 2-page essay explaining your academic preparation, research interests, and the specific Michigan Tech faculty members with whom you want to work. You can organize, structure, and polish your narrative using the [Abroaducate SOP Builder](/sop).
3. **Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Highlight technical engineering competencies, software proficiency (MATLAB, Python, CAD, C++), laboratory techniques, published work, and relevant industry internships.
4. **Three Letters of Recommendation:** Recommenders submit their evaluations digitally via automated email links generated by the application portal.
5. **English Language Proficiency:** Demonstrated via TOEFL iBT (minimum 79), IELTS (minimum 6.5), or Duolingo English Test (minimum 110). Applicants who earned a post-secondary degree from an officially English-medium university may request a departmental language waiver.
6. **GRE General Test:** Most engineering and computing departments at Michigan Tech have made the GRE **optional** or have waived it completely for Master's and PhD applicants, removing another substantial application expense.
