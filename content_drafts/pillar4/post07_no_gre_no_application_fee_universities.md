Applying to graduate school in the United States has traditionally been one of the most expensive academic endeavors in the world.

Before an applicant ever receives an admission decision or an offer of financial aid, the mandatory upfront testing and application fees routinely create an insurmountable barrier:
- **The Graduate Record Examination (GRE):** Costs **$220** per registration, not including prep materials, practice tests, and additional score report dispatch fees ($35 per university after the first four).
- **Institutional Application Fees:** Range from **$75 to $130** per university.

For an international student applying to six research universities, the combined cost of GRE registration, score dispatches, and application submission fees easily exceeds **$800 to $1,000**.

To democratize graduate education and evaluate applicants on authentic academic competence rather than test-taking wealth, a growing number of distinguished US research institutions have instituted a **Double Waiver Policy**:
1. **No GRE Required:** Standardized tests are either permanently waived or entirely test-optional across major departments.
2. **$0 Application Fee:** Applications are submitted completely free of charge, with no coupon codes or financial paperwork required.

Below, we detail 8 accredited American universities that offer the complete Double Waiver advantage, verify whether students admitted without GRE scores qualify for graduate assistantships, and outline the step-by-step application process.

---

## 1. The Double Waiver Advantage: Why It Matters

Securing admission without testing and submission fees offers two distinct strategic benefits:

- **Resource Reallocation:** By saving $800+ on upfront testing and submission fees, applicants can direct their budget toward essential credential evaluations, official certified document translations, and initial visa compliance fees.
- **Holistic Review Dominance:** In the absence of standardized testing scores, admissions committees place far greater emphasis on your undergraduate academic transcripts, technical project portfolio, Statement of Purpose, and letters of recommendation.

Crucially: **Waiving the GRE does not diminish your funding eligibility.** In virtually all institutions listed below, Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) and Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) are awarded based on faculty interviews, departmental coursework evaluation, and technical background—not standardized test percentiles.

## 2. Top 8 US Universities with No GRE and No Application Fee

The following universities offer accredited graduate degrees (Master's and PhD) with permanent or structural fee-free applications and test-optional/test-free admissions:

### 1. Michigan Technological University (MTU)
- **Application Fee:** $0 permanent automatic waiver for all graduate applications.
- **GRE Policy:** GRE is optional or waived across the vast majority of graduate programs in the College of Engineering and College of Computing.
- **Funding Opportunities:** Generous GTA and GRA appointments offering full tuition coverage and competitive monthly research stipends.

### 2. University of Dayton
- **Application Fee:** $0 permanent automatic waiver across all graduate departments.
- **GRE Policy:** Waived for numerous master's degrees, including Computer Science, Software Engineering, and specialized engineering tracks.
- **Funding Opportunities:** Departmental graduate assistantships and international merit tuition discount awards.

### 3. Case Western Reserve University (Select Graduate Programs)
- **Application Fee:** $0 for designated graduate departments upon participation in virtual open houses or automatic departmental exemptions.
- **GRE Policy:** Completely test-optional across numerous biomedical, engineering, and data science disciplines.
- **Reputation:** A top-40 private research university renowned for healthcare, biomedical engineering, and computational biology.

### 4. University of Saint Thomas (Minnesota)
- **Application Fee:** $0 application fee for graduate degree programs.
- **GRE Policy:** GRE is waived for Master's programs in Software Engineering, Data Science, and Information Technology.
- **Industry Connections:** Direct corporate recruitment ties with Fortune 500 corporations across the Minneapolis-Saint Paul metropolitan corridor.

### 5. Illinois Institute of Technology (Illinois Tech)
- **Application Fee:** Permanent $0 application fee for Master's and PhD applications submitted through the institutional portal.
- **GRE Policy:** GRE is waived for students applying to Master's programs across engineering, computer science, and architecture who meet minimum undergraduate GPA standards.
- **Funding Opportunities:** Institutional tuition scholarships awarded automatically upon academic review.

### 6. Loyola University Chicago
- **Application Fee:** $0 application fee for graduate degree applications across Arts & Sciences and Business.
- **GRE Policy:** GRE/GMAT waived for programs in Computer Science, Information Technology, and Healthcare Administration.
- **Advantage:** Located in downtown Chicago with robust corporate internship placement.

### 7. University of New Haven
- **Application Fee:** Waived during virtual graduate recruitment events and international partner cycles.
- **GRE Policy:** GRE completely waived for all Master of Science programs in the Tagliatela College of Engineering.
- **Funding Opportunities:** Dean's Scholarship awards granting up to 50% tuition reduction to admitted international scholars.

### 8. Western Illinois University (WIU)
- **Application Fee:** Waived during international recruitment priority periods and specific preview cycles.
- **GRE Policy:** No GRE required for master's programs in Computer Science, Applied Statistics, and Chemistry.
- **Funding Opportunities:** Full-tuition remission Graduate Assistantships across campus departments.

## 3. How to Make Your Application Stand Out Without a GRE Score

When standardized testing is eliminated from the admissions equation, other components of your dossier carry significantly greater evaluative weight:

1. **Upper-Division Coursework Grades:** Highlight your performance in 300- and 400-level core subjects (e.g., Data Structures, Operating Systems, Thermodynamics, Econometrics) rather than generic introductory electives.
2. **A Tailored Statement of Purpose:** Clearly connect your undergraduate coursework, capstone projects, and career goals with specific laboratories and faculty research at the target institution. Structure your essay using the [Abroaducate SOP Builder](/sop).
3. **Evidence of Independent Research or Practical Projects:** Include links to your GitHub repositories, research abstracts, published papers, or portfolio links directly in your resume and application form.
4. **Substantive Academic Recommendations:** Ensure your recommenders highlight your analytical rigor, independent problem-solving skills, and laboratory competence.

## 4. Required Academic Documentation & Translations

Even without application fees and GRE scores, institutional admissions standards remain rigorous:

1. **Official Transcripts & Degree Certificates:** Clear scans of your complete post-secondary academic records. For documents in languages other than English, certified word-for-word translations are mandatory. You can obtain certified translations compliant with US graduate admissions requirements via [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).
2. **English Language Proficiency Proof:** Submitting a strong TOEFL iBT, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test score demonstrates your verbal and written readiness for graduate study and qualifies you for Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA) roles.
3. **Academic Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Highlighting technical proficiencies, programming languages, analytical software, and research initiatives.

## 5. Cross-Border Financial Management for Incoming Students

Once admitted with a $0 application fee and no GRE expenses, your primary financial milestone becomes securing your Form I-20 and planning your student relocation.

While departmental assistantships often cover tuition and provide living stipends, self-funded candidates and incoming scholars must manage visa application fees, flight bookings, and housing deposits.
