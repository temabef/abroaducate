Sweden is widely recognized as a global leader in sustainability, gender equality, software engineering, and technological innovation. Institutions such as KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Lund University, Uppsala University, and Chalmers University of Technology attract the world's most capable minds.

For non-EU/EEA international students, Swedish university tuition fees range from **SEK 80,000 to SEK 295,000 per academic year** (approximately $8,000 to $28,000 USD).

However, the Swedish government funds one of the most generous and prestigious postgraduate fellowship programs in the world: the **Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP)**.

The SI Scholarship provides:
- **100% Full Tuition Coverage:** Directly paid to your Swedish university.
- **Monthly Living Allowance:** **SEK 12,000 per month** (approximately $1,150 USD) disbursed throughout the entire one- or two-year master's program.
- **Travel Grant:** A one-time allowance of **SEK 15,000** for travel to and from Sweden.
- **Comprehensive Medical & Accident Insurance:** Provided through the Swedish State Insurance Agency (Kammarkollegiet).
- **Network for Global Professionals (NFGP):** Exclusive access to leadership workshops, diplomatic events, and alumni networks across Scandinavia.

Unlike purely academic awards, the Swedish Institute evaluates candidates primarily on their **demonstrated leadership potential, societal commitment, and documented professional work history**.

Below, we break down the critical **3,000-hour work experience requirement**, the exact structure of the mandatory SI Leadership and Work templates, and how to avoid the administrative errors that eliminate over 60% of applicants each year.

## 1. The 3,000-Hour Work Experience Requirement Explained

![Desk flatlay with Swedish Institute application documents, certified employment certificate, and laptop]({{BODY_IMAGE_URL}})

To be eligible for the SI Scholarship, applicants must possess a minimum of **3,000 documented hours of work experience** prior to the scholarship application deadline.

### How to Calculate 3,000 Hours
The Swedish Institute uses a standard 40-hour full-time work week as its evaluative benchmark:
- **Full-Time Work:** 40 hours per week $\times$ 50 working weeks per year = **2,000 hours per year**. Therefore, 3,000 hours equates to approximately **one year and nine months (21 months)** of full-time employment.
- **Part-Time Work:** If you worked 20 hours per week, 3,000 hours requires approximately **38 months** of continuous employment.

### What Counts as Work Experience?
The Swedish Institute is remarkably broad in its definition of professional experience:
1. **Full-time and Part-time Employment:** Paid corporate, public sector, or NGO employment.
2. **Mandatory National Service / Internship:** Mandatory civil service programs (such as Nigeria's NYSC, Ghana's NSS, or clinical internships) qualify fully, provided they occurred after secondary school.
3. **Freelance & Self-Employment:** Documented through client contracts, invoices, and business tax registrations.
4. **Voluntary Work:** Leadership or community volunteer positions in registered civic organizations can contribute toward your total hours.

## 2. The Mandatory SI Application Templates

The Swedish Institute does not accept generic letters of recommendation or unstructured employer references. **You must use the official, unedited SI PDF/Word templates** released annually on the SI website in January.

Submitting any external template or altering the margins, tables, or formatting of the official forms results in **instant, automated disqualification**.

### 1. Proof of Work Experience Form
- You must have your current or former employer complete and sign this form.
- The form requires exact dates (DD/MM/YYYY), hours worked per week, total calculated hours, specific job descriptions, and the organization's official stamp.
- If you accumulated 3,000 hours across multiple employers, you may submit multiple forms (up to a maximum of three).

### 2. Proof of Leadership Form
- The Swedish Institute defines leadership broadly: managing a team, mentoring colleagues, taking initiative on a community project, or driving institutional policy changes.
- Your referee must provide concrete examples answering:
  - What was the specific leadership challenge or initiative?
  - What actions did the applicant take?
  - What tangible, measurable results were achieved?

### 3. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Must be created using the official SI CV template (maximum three pages). Any additional pages will cause your application to be rejected.

## 3. The 2-Step Application Procedure

Applying for the SI Scholarship requires coordinating two independent application portals:

### Step 1: Apply for University Admission on Universityadmissions.se
- **Deadline:** Mid-October to **mid-January**.
- Create an account on the national Swedish admissions portal, **Universityadmissions.se**.
- Apply for up to four eligible master's programs.
- Pay the non-refundable Swedish university application fee of **SEK 900** (or provide proof of exemption) before the February 1 documentation deadline.
- Receive your unique **8-digit Application Number**.

### Step 2: Apply for the SI Scholarship on the SI Portal
- **Application Window:** Mid-February (typically open for 10 to 14 days only).
- Access the dedicated SI application portal.
- Input your 8-digit Universityadmissions.se application number.
- Upload your completed SI CV, Proof of Work Experience form(s), Proof of Leadership form, and valid passport scan.

## 4. Academic Documentation & Official Translations

Before your university dossier can be reviewed by Swedish central admissions:

1. **Certified Transcripts & Diplomas:** Scanned copies of your original degree certificates and complete semester mark sheets.
2. **Official Certified Translations:** If your academic records, employment verification letters, or tax documents were issued in a language other than English, Swedish, or German, you must provide certified word-for-word translations. You can secure certified document translations that meet Swedish university and consular standards via [ImmiTranslate](https://immitranslate.com/?ref=543dj3rd).
3. **English Proficiency Requirements:** Most Swedish master's programs require English 6 level, verified by IELTS Academic (minimum 6.5, no band below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT (minimum 90, 20 in writing).
