# Abroaducate Pillar 2 Content Clusters & Publishing Blueprint (October 2026)

## Overview & Editorial Standards
This blueprint outlines the 12 targeted, high-intent SEO articles scheduled for October 2026 across 3 strategic clusters. The goal is to capture high-converting search traffic from international students actively applying for Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 university intakes, driving organic clicks to Abroaducate's platform tools (Statement of Purpose AI Generator, Program Finder, and Scholarship Radar).

### Editorial Guidelines
1. **Tone & Style:** Practical, grounded, and written in a natural, human voice. Avoid robotic transitions or formal corporate fluff.
2. **Zero Emojis:** Strictly no emojis in titles, headings, bullet points, callouts, or body text.
3. **Actionable Details:** Every article must include verified university names, real deadlines, eligibility requirements, and application portals.
4. **Internal CTAs:** Natural contextual integration of Abroaducate features (`/sop`, `/programs`, `/scholarships`).
5. **Affiliate Monetization Integration:** Every post strategically embeds relevant partner links to drive clicks towards our 1,000 monthly clicks goal:
