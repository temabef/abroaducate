## NOTES FOR SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER

### X (Twitter) Best Practices:
- Keep under 280 characters (including spaces)
- Use emojis for visual appeal (but not too many)
- Include 2-3 relevant hashtags
- Link goes at the end
- Use line breaks for readability
- Call to action: "Apply now", "Don't miss out", etc.

### LinkedIn Best Practices:
- Longer format (300-600 characters is optimal)
- Professional tone but engaging
- Use bullet points for benefits
- Include more context about the scholarship
- 3-5 hashtags at the end
- Can include a personal note or success story
- Call to action with direct link

### General Tips:
- Post during peak hours (8-10 AM, 12-2 PM, 5-7 PM local time)
- Use location tags when possible
- Engage with comments quickly
- Repost with slight variations if no deadline pressure
- Track which posts get most engagement

## ENGAGEMENT TIPS

1. **Pin important deadlines** to top of profile
2. **Create urgency** for scholarships with close deadlines
3. **Use carousel posts** on LinkedIn for multi-scholarship roundups
4. **Tag universities** when posting (increases reach)
5. **Quote tweet** successful applicants' stories
6. **Create threads** on X for detailed scholarship guides
7. **Use Abroaducate branding** - mention your platform link in comments
