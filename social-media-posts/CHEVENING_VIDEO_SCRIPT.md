# Chevening Scholarship Video Script

**Duration:** 60-90 seconds  
**Tone:** Direct, practical, no-nonsense  
**Format:** Talking head with B-roll or text overlays

---

## SCRIPT

[HOOK - First 3 seconds]

"The UK government is funding 1,500 master's degrees this year. Here's what you need to know."

[MAIN CONTENT - 50 seconds]

"Chevening covers everything: full tuition, £1,300 monthly stipend, flights, and visa costs. You pick any UK university, any master's program.

The catch? Two years of work experience. Not internships—actual professional work.

Here's what actually wins:

Show measurable impact. 'I led 5 volunteers to deliver clean water to 200 families' beats generic statements about passion.

Apply to three UK universities. You need ONE unconditional offer by July 8, 2027.

Two common mistakes that kill applications:

One—choosing three random courses just to fill the requirement. Pick programs you actually want.

Two—missing the July 8 deadline for your unconditional offer. The scholarship is worthless without it.

Applications open August 4, close October 6. Interviews happen March-April. Results in June.

50,000 alumni across 160 countries. Presidents, ministers, industry leaders. This network matters more than the degree."

[CTA - Last 7 seconds]

"Full application guide, eligibility checker, and essay tips on Abroaducate. Link in description."

## ALTERNATIVE SHORTER VERSION (30 seconds)

"Chevening: UK government's flagship scholarship. 1,500 spots, fully funded.

Requirements: Bachelor's degree, two years work experience, apply to three UK universities.

What wins: specific leadership examples with numbers. What you did, how many people, what changed.

Applications close October 6. You need an unconditional university offer by July 8.

Full guide at Abroaducate.com/scholarships/chevening"

---

## TALKING POINTS TO EMPHASIZE

✓ Full funding including stipend  
✓ Complete freedom to choose university and program  
✓ Two-year work experience requirement  
✓ Three-course application strategy  
✓ July 8 offer deadline is critical  
✓ Alumni network value  

## AVOID THESE PHRASES

✗ "Dream of studying in the UK"  
✗ "Life-changing opportunity"  
✗ "Prestigious scholarship"  
✗ "Once in a lifetime"  
✗ "Transform your future"  
✗ "Passionate about"  

## INSTEAD USE

✓ "Here's what you need"  
✓ "This is how it works"  
✓ "Funded master's program"  
✓ "1,500 available spots"  
✓ "Specific requirements"  
✓ "Show measurable results"
